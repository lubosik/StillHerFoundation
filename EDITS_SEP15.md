## 1. The logo is rejected

Her words: "I don't like that the stem of the H goes through the S. It's giving money sign and I don't like it. It's giving back vibes."

The dollar sign read was a misreading of the earlier meeting. She does not want it.

What she does want:
- A "genius moment". Her reference is a friend's logo for Crippled Cutie: a heart with a crystal bandaid over it. She wants the equivalent conceptual leap.
- Two marks: an emblem, and a lockup with the words. "You know how you have RR and it says at the bottom Rosen Relations. Same." Also Chanel's two C's for a step and repeat.
- The emblem is SHF with "The Still Her Foundation" set around or under it.
- It has to be cute, and it has to be legible as an emblem at small size.

Concepts she ruled out, in her own words:
- A silhouette of a woman. Easy as an emblem but "you're just not going to be able to tell what that is" once it is an outline of figures.
- Water. "It doesn't correlate to what I'm doing."
- A heel. "That could come off as a makeup brand."
- Foundation as in a building's foundations. Reads as architecture, construction or an AI company.

Colour: the emblem renders as a darker turquoise than the rest of the page. "I need the colour address to be the same." Unify emblem and site colour.
