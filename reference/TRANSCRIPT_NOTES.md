It's the inside sliver that's going to be gold."

## 3.2 The emblem and wordmark

**Two logos are required.** Nani: "So we need two logos. We need a written one and a picture one."

**Chanel logic.** Her explicit structural reference: "you know how Chanel has the two C's and then it also has Chanel and then it also has the two C's and Chanel's under it like the whole shaboodle". So: an interlocking monogram mark, a wordmark, and a lockup of the monogram with the wordmark beneath it.

**Typographic treatment of the wordmark.** "Still" set plain, "Her" set in a fancy or cursive hand: "I want it to be like the still like the still and then her... Her is written fancy, like the Still Her Foundation." The earlier part of the same sentence is garbled by Fathom but the settled instruction is: STILL regular, HER fancy/cursive, then FOUNDATION.

**The monogram: SHF, interlocked.** Worked out live on Sep 13 @42:44 to @47:00 by drawing on paper to camera. The construction she landed on:
- The **S** is "Still", drawn with a long extended stem.
- That stem "is going to serve as the middle part that connects the H as well as the top line that forms the F."
- The **H** is "Her", the **F** is "Foundation".
- Her final summary: "the S goes down with a long... because the is going to serve as the middle part that connects the H as well as the top line that forms the F. Pulling the stem down and bringing that back. There you go. Still her foundation."
- Both men note it reads like a dollar sign and like a chain: "It kind of reminds you of the dollar sign", "That would be a good chain, to be honest. That would be fire."
- Letter sizing: roughly equal. "it should be the same, roughly the same size."

**Emblem styling:** "make it elegant, like make it elegant... If you got to add filigree, add filigree, make it feminine and romantic, but still strong."

**Coin / medallion variant for the tournament.** "I'm going to have the Still Her Foundation as the fat part of the coin, and then I wrote the Good Guys tournament in letters around it." So the SHF monogram sits at the centre of a circular medallion with event wording running around the ring. The foundation's own emblem uses the same mark **without** the event wording: "But without the good guys tournament... literally, I just need that." Lubosi's suggestion during the sketching: "What if you put a circle around it?"

**Why the emblem matters to her:** she rejected the previous concept because it could not be carved. "when you look at an emblem, if I want to turn this into an emblem, I can't have them carve out like three..." (referring to three generations of women). "that's why the picture that we're going to carve out is so important."
