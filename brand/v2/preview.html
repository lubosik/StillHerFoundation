The Still Her Foundation, emblem v2

Five emblem concepts, each a separate proposition rather than a variation. No vertical stroke passes through an S anywhere in this set. Every mark is hand built path geometry on a 200 by 200 grid, carries no type, and uses only the palette defined in src/styles/tokens.css.

The five concepts

Shown at 256px, 64px, 32px and 16px, on cream and on ink. The small sizes are the test that matters. Rationales are in CONCEPTS.md.

The selected mark, in production

Her Brooch, shipped as four files in public/brand/. The words in both lockups are outlines drawn from the project's own font files, so neither depends on a font being present.
